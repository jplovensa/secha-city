# Chapter 01 characters

- Studio cast. Based on [Maya, Locomotion Collection](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/locomotion-collection-maya-3d-person-animated-1499b2fd) by Innerscene, adapted from MakeHuman/MPFB assets. The model includes a weighted humanoid skeleton and authored animation. SECHA City compresses the browser asset and applies costume variants for its fictionalised game cast.
- Garden visitor. [Hiker v1, LOD0](https://tevy.dev/models/hiker) by tevy, with a weighted humanoid skeleton and looping animation.

Both source assets are under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
