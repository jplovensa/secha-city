# Ruang Bayang artwork

SECHA City’s relief adapts four wayang kulit puppet photographs from the Tropenmuseum collection, via Wikimedia Commons.

- [Kresna, TM 4685-95](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:COLLECTIE_TROPENMUSEUM_Wajangpop_voorstellende_Kresna_TMnr_4685-95.jpg)
- [Raden Arjuna, TM 8-307](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:COLLECTIE_TROPENMUSEUM_Wajang_kulit_pop_voorstellende_Raden_Arjuna_TMnr_8-307.jpg)
- [Bima, TM 8-265](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:COLLECTIE_TROPENMUSEUM_Wajang_kulit_pop_voorstellende_Bima_TMnr_8-265.jpg)
- [Yudhistira, TM 8-264](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:COLLECTIE_TROPENMUSEUM_Wajang_kulit_pop_voorstellende_Yudhistira_TMnr_8-264.jpg)

The photographs were cropped, traced and translated into monochrome surface detail and cut silhouettes. The gunungan is an original drawing. The adapted wayang artwork is available under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). The license applies to the wayang artwork.
